# Midnight Semester Story Bible

## Logline

A final-year engineering student stays behind alone on campus to finish a project during semester break and discovers the university has been quietly finishing something else since 2005.

## World

- University: Ravenswood Institute of Technology
- Location: fictional campus outside a mid-sized Indian city
- Year: 2026
- Player: Aarav Mehta, final-year Data Science student

## Core Mystery

In 2004, Meera Iyer volunteered for an Applied Cognition Lab trial to pay hostel fees. In 2005, she disappeared from official records. Block A was sealed, the basement lab was reclassified as storage, and institutional silence turned negligence into folklore.

## Acts

1. Arrival: rainy campus, locked gate, backup power routes into Block A.
2. Descent: Academic Block, Library, Hostel, case-file collection, first Meera encounter.
3. Basement: Applied Cognition Lab, Dr. Verma confession, non-combat final choice.

## Endings

- Report It: truth reaches authorities or press.
- Trust Kulkarni: evidence vanishes into the institution.
- Stay: the player remains with Meera.
- Run: rushed escape with unresolved truth.
